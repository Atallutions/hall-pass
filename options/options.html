Block the sites that distract you, and step out when you have to.

Like a hall pass at school, it lets you leave during focus time, but not often and not for long.

How it works

  1. Pick the sites

    Add the sites that distract you and sort them into categories.

  2. Focus

    During focus time, those sites show a block page instead.

  3. Take a pass

    The site opens for 5 minutes, then Hall Pass blocks it again.

The rules

6passes per site, per day
5 minper pass
00:00when the counts reset

There's no setting to change them, so you can't raise them while you're distracted.

The modes

SchedulesIn use Blocks your sites at set times every week. You don't have to start anything. Sessions and breaksIn use Blocks your sites for a session you start, then opens them for a break. Good for the Pomodoro technique.

Your list and settings stay in this browser. Hall Pass doesn't send any data.

Uncategorized

Sites you add from the toolbar popup go here. Use the menu next to a site to move it into a category.

    Focus time

    Hall Pass blocks your sites during focus time. Choose how focus time starts.

    Schedules

    Hall Pass blocks your sites while any enabled schedule is running. A schedule can run past midnight, for example 22:00 to 06:00.

    Sessions and breaks

    Session
    Break

    After each break, the next session starts on its own. This repeats until you end the session or close the browser. A running session keeps its lengths, and new ones apply to the next session you start.
