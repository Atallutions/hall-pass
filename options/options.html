How it works

  1. Add the sites that distract you. Adding a site also blocks its subdomains, so reddit.com covers www.reddit.com.
  2. The extension blocks them while an enabled schedule is active. Opening one of them shows the block page instead of the site.
  3. You can take a 5-minute pass. Use the toolbar popup or the block page. Each site gets 6 passes a day, and the count resets at midnight.

You can't end a pass early, and removing a site doesn't reset its count. The limits are fixed on purpose.
